MY
STORY

I’ve spent my career at the intersection of technology, industry, and government—building connections, shaping ecosystems, and turning ideas into meaningful action.

From engineering and industry leadership to leading GTech and working with the Government of Kerala, every chapter has shaped how I see people, ideas, and the systems around us.

Today, as Head of External Affairs at IBS Software, I focus on building relationships, creating opportunities, and helping technology make a meaningful impact.

But beyond titles and milestones, I believe it’s the connections we build along the way that define who we are. The stories we create, the people we meet, and the lives we touch are what ultimately remain.

So, I’m here to keep exploring, keep connecting, and keep living life to the fullest—while building what matters.
